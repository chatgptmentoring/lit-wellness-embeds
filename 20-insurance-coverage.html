Good to know

Common questions

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

In many cases, no. If your insurance plan does require a referral, we’ll help you sort that out before you begin.

How do I check whether I’m covered?

Tap Check My Coverage, choose Get your estimate, and enter your insurance information. Once you see you’re covered, set up your first consultation.

What if insurance isn’t a fit?

We’ll talk through self-pay options and help you choose the most practical path. We accept credit cards and HSA, HRA or FSA cards.

Are sessions only virtual?

Sessions are virtual, so you can join from anywhere in the U.S. If you live near our home office, in-person sessions are welcome too.

Ready to see what your plan covers?

It only takes a couple of minutes. Not sure where to start? Book a free strategy session and we’ll figure out the best next step together.
