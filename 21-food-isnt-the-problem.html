Tanya’s signature framework

Name It. Claim It. Reframe It.

When a craving hits and you’re not physically hungry, three steps help you respond with intention instead of reacting on autopilot.

1

Name It

Notice what your body is telling you and put a specific word to the feeling.

2

Claim It

Own the feeling without judgment. Emotions aren’t right or wrong — they’re messengers.

3

Reframe It

Look at the facts, find a kinder and truer thought, and choose what you actually need.

Name what you feel · Claim what you think · Reframe based on truth · Respond with intention

Practical tools

Tools you can use today

The Hunger/Fullness Scale

Check in with your body before you eat, so you can tell physical hunger from emotional hunger.

The Dictionary of Cravings

Crunchy, creamy, sweet or chewy? Decode what the texture you crave says about how you feel.

Your Dopamine Menu

A personal list of non-food ways to soothe, reward and recharge — ready before you need it.

The “Yellow Light”

Spot the early warning signs and questions that let you pause before a craving takes over.

Diaphragmatic breathing

Use your body’s built-in brake pedal to calm the stress response in a few slow breaths.

The Intentional Pause

Five minutes between the impulse and the action. Most urges fade — and you get to choose.
