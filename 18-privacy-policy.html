    Information we collect

    The Tanya AI coach

    Tanya AI is an artificial-intelligence coach trained on Tanya’s book, Food Isn’t the Problem, and her approach. It is an AI, not a person, and it is not medical, nutrition, or mental-health care. Before your first message, we ask you to agree to a short notice.

    How it works

    • Your message is sent to our chat service, hosted by Supabase on servers located in Ireland (European Union).
    • To write a reply, your message and recent conversation are sent to OpenAI, which generates the response. Under OpenAI’s API terms, data sent through its API is not used to train OpenAI’s models, and OpenAI may retain it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse.
    • The conversation is saved in our Supabase database.

    Who can see your chats

    LIT Wellness Solutions and people working on our behalf may review saved conversations to improve the coach, check that it is giving safe and accurate information, and respond to any safety concerns. Conversations that mention self-harm or similar risks are automatically flagged so they can be reviewed. The chat is anonymous unless you type identifying details — so please don’t share your name, contact information or medical records in the chat.

    How long chats are kept

    Chats are automatically deleted 12 months after the last message. Rate-limiting data is deleted within one day. You can ask us to delete your chat sooner (see Your choices and rights); it helps if you tell us roughly when you chatted and what you asked.

    On your device

    The chat window stores your current conversation in your browser’s session storage (cleared when you close the tab) and remembers that you accepted the chat notice in local storage. It does not set advertising cookies.

    Health information and HIPAA

    Our website, forms and Tanya AI coach are for general information and education. They are not used to provide treatment and are not part of a client’s medical record. HIPAA protects health information that health-care providers create and keep as part of providing care; information you choose to share through a public website form or the AI coach is generally not covered by HIPAA — which is one more reason to keep private health details out of them.

    If you are a client, please share health information through the secure client portal or during your sessions, not through website forms, social media, or the AI coach.
